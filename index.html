    Getting them into the tune

    • Learn each line slowly with the loop on, then take it up to tune tempo. Sing the last two notes of every bar; that is the voice leading you are after.
    • Match the line to the rhythm of your chart. Where the ii and the V share a bar, use the one-bar lines; where each chord gets a full bar, use the two-bar ones.
    • Move the start point. Begin a line on beat 3 of the previous bar, or drop the first two notes, so it doesn't always sound like an exercise.
    • Mix halves. The ii half of one line and the V half of another almost always connect, because each bar ends a step away from a chord tone.
    • Hit New variations to get different lines for the same ii–Vs. Tick Keep on the lines you like and they stay put.
    • Each line has its own octave (Low, Orig, High) next to it, and a Print box: untick the lines you don't want on paper, then Print.
